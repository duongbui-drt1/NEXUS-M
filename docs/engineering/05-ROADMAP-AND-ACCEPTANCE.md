# NEXUS M — Roadmap & Acceptance

## Phase 0 — Repository/ecosystem audit
Deliver repository inventory, build/test/CI baseline, dependency inventory, C integration map, security/risk register, prioritized findings and Phase 1 decision backlog. **Gate:** evidence-backed report reviewed; no feature coding.

## Phase 1 — Product/domain/architecture freeze
Deliver approved boundaries, context model, integration contracts, authorization model, metric semantics, NFRs and ADRs. **Gate:** critical ownership/security questions resolved.

## Phase 2 — Management foundation
App shell, context resolution, module registry, entitlement-aware navigation and audit foundation.

## Phase 3 — Data/integration/KPI foundation
Versioned contracts, ingestion, metric catalog, lineage, freshness, quality and reconciliation.

## Phase 4 — Executive intelligence
Business health dashboard, KPI views, alerts and safe drill-down.

## Phase 5 — Business intelligence
Analytics, report builder, saved/scheduled reports and controlled exports.

## Phase 6 — Workflow and approval
Versioned workflow, tasks, approvals, delegation, escalation and immutable decision history.

## Phase 7 — Organization and people overview
Configurable org structure, assignments and workforce overview.

## Phase 8 — Risk and compliance
Risk register, controls, obligations, evidence and deadline alerts.

## Phase 9 — Corporate reporting and resource/performance
Board reporting, objectives, targets, initiatives and portfolio views.

## Phase 10 — Optional AI
Permission-aware, cited and observable AI assistance with human approval controls.

## Phase 11 — Production hardening
Security assessment, performance baseline, backup/restore evidence, migration/release runbooks, alerting and incident procedures.

## Definition of Done
Acceptance criteria documented; unit/integration/relevant E2E tests pass; tenant isolation and authorization tests pass; API/schema/migration impact reviewed; errors/audit/telemetry appropriate; accessibility/localization and UI states handled; docs updated; no unresolved critical/high security issue. No production-readiness claim without reproducible evidence.

Use status labels Proposed / In Progress / Blocked / Verified. Verified requires evidence such as test output, CI result, contract test or reviewed artifact.
