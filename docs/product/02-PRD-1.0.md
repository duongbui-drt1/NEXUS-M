# NEXUS M — Product Requirements Document 1.0

**Status:** Proposed baseline; validate during Phase 1.

## Personas
Owner, board member, CEO, C-level executive, director, department manager, team leader, employee, auditor and external advisor.

## Functional requirements

### M00 — Management Foundation
Provide app shell, active workspace/org context, entitlement- and permission-aware module navigation, localization, preferences, search, notifications and activity timeline.

### M01 — Executive Intelligence
Show business health, strategic KPIs, trends and alerts. Support valid period/org comparisons and permission-aware drill-down. Expose source, freshness and metric definition.

### M02 — Business Intelligence
Maintain versioned metric definitions. Support analytics, report creation, saved/scheduled reports and export. Preserve filters, units, currency, time basis, lineage and export permissions. Flag stale or low-quality data.

### M03 — Workflow & Approval
Support versioned workflows, controlled transitions, routing, delegation, escalation, SLA, tasks, comments and evidence. Preserve immutable decision history. Enforce idempotency, concurrency and separation of duties. Orchestrate approvals without owning source transactions.

### M04 — Organization & People
Support configurable units, departments, teams, positions and reporting lines; effective-dated assignments; workforce overview. Not full HR/payroll by default.

### M05 — Risk & Governance
Maintain risk registers, assessments, owners, controls, incidents, mitigation plans and evidence, with history.

### M06 — Compliance Intelligence
Track obligations, jurisdictions, deadlines, evidence and alerts. Identify source and validation status of regulatory content.

### M07 — Board & Corporate Reporting
Create versioned board/periodic reports, review and approval workflows, secure distribution and historical snapshots.

### M08 — Resource & Performance Management
Track objectives, OKRs, KPI targets, initiatives, portfolios and budget responsibility without becoming the accounting ledger.

### M09 — AI Executive Intelligence
Provide optional grounded assistance. Respect authorization; cite sources, timestamps and metric definitions. Track provider/model/usage/cost. Require human confirmation for consequential actions.

### M10 — Data & Integration Hub
Manage connectors, ingestion, event consumption and normalization. Track lineage, freshness, quality, reconciliation and failures. Support retry, dead-letter handling and controlled replay.

## Non-functional requirements
Tenant isolation; server-side authorization; auditability; reliable/idempotent background work; observable APIs/jobs/integrations; backup/restore and migration safety; accessible desktop-first UI; localization of dates/numbers/timezones/currencies; export and retention controls. Define performance targets after workload discovery.

## MVP acceptance
C integration is contract-tested; organization context is secure; metrics expose definition/source/freshness; dashboards enforce access scope; workflows retain decision history; exports respect permissions; tenant isolation tests pass; telemetry and recovery procedures are documented.

## Explicit exclusions
No full ERP, accounting ledger, logistics execution engine, full HR/payroll, autonomous approvals or direct cross-app database access.
