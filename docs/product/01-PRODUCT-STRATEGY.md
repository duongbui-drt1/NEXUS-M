# NEXUS M — Product Strategy

## Positioning
A universal enterprise management and decision-intelligence platform connecting organizational context, trusted metrics, management workflows and accountable decisions.

## Target organizations
Micro-business, SME, mid-market, enterprise and enterprise groups. Adapt through progressive module activation, configurable structures and entitlement-aware modules. Do not force small organizations into complex corporate hierarchies.

## Product promise
Help owners, executives and managers understand performance, coordinate decisions and trace why decisions were made.

## Principles
1. One source of truth per business domain.
2. Management intelligence does not replace operational systems.
3. Configurable complexity with simple defaults.
4. Permission-aware by design.
5. Metrics must be explainable, fresh and traceable.
6. Human accountability for consequential decisions.
7. AI is optional, governed and auditable.
8. Integrate through versioned contracts, never database coupling.

## Ecosystem
C provides platform identity, tenancy, access and entitlements. M owns management orchestration/intelligence. F owns finance/accounting. E owns logistics/transport execution.

## Proposed MVP
C integration and workspace/org context; configurable organization overview; KPI catalog with definitions/source/freshness/lineage; executive dashboard with permission-aware drill-down; basic analytics and saved reports; basic workflow/approval inbox and audit history; notifications and management reports; observability and security controls.

## Exclusions from MVP
Full ERP, accounting ledger, logistics execution engine, full HR/payroll, CRM replacement, legal contract lifecycle management, autonomous AI approvals and premature microservice decomposition.

## Success measurement
Set numeric targets only after baseline measurement. Track activation, time-to-first-useful-dashboard, metric freshness/quality, report preparation time, approval cycle time, integration reliability, authorization defects and user trust.
